Simple for firms. Simple for clients.

Here is how our seamless partnership model operates to bring white-labeled giving to your practice.

1

Your firm introduces charitable giving as part of the client conversation.

Incorporate philanthropic strategy into estate planning, tax planning, business sales, or generational wealth transfers without worrying about processing overhead.

2

Clients access a branded giving experience connected to your firm.

Clients log into a modern portal featuring your brand's colors and logo, keeping your firm front-and-center during their giving journey.

3

GiveWise and FlightPath handle administration, processing, compliance, and support behind the scenes.

From complex donation receipting and tax compliance reporting to sending grants to chosen charities, we ensure operational excellence.

4

Advisors remain central to the relationship while clients gain a flexible charitable giving solution.

You keep full client visibility and AUM options while offering institutional-grade donor-advised funds (DAFs).
